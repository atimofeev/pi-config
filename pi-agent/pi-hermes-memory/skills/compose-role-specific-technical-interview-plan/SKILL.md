## When to Use
Use when designing a reusable technical hiring interview with multiple competency sections and optional candidate-specific tailoring.

## Procedure
1. Read role requirements, recruitment guidance and candidate screening; separate confirmed information from claims and missing evidence.
2. Choose an explicit duration and reserve candidate Q&A and wrap-up before allocating technical blocks. Resolve source timing conflicts openly.
3. Design a small set of realistic scenarios for each required section. Include main ask, optional probes, strong evidence and unsafe signals; make probes alternatives, not hidden mandatory questions.
4. Separate reusable criteria from candidate tailoring. Reuse screening facts without repeating screening; missing resume keywords mean unknown experience, not incompetence.
5. Use anchored section scores with Not assessed distinct from failure. Record evidence, uncertainty and job-related rationale; do not invent hiring thresholds or feedback deadlines.
6. Verify timings sum to advertised slot, required topics are covered and local document links resolve.

## Pitfalls
- A short interview samples competence; defer unresolved critical areas to focused follow-up instead of overrunning candidate Q&A.
- Product familiarity does not substitute for safe operational reasoning.
- Do not claim resumes demonstrate skills independently; distinguish reported outcomes from observed reasoning.

## Verification
1. Agenda arithmetic matches total duration and technical core.
2. Each required section has realistically limited main scenarios and clear evaluation signals.
3. Source policy, proposed adaptations and candidate-specific tailoring remain distinguishable.
